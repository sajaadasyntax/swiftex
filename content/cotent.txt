Power Flushing

Restore the performance of your heating system.

Over time, central heating systems can build up sludge, rust and debris inside the pipework and radiators. This buildup can reduce heating efficiency, cause cold spots in radiators and place unnecessary strain on your boiler.

At Swiftfix Plumbing & Heating, we provide professional power flushing services to clean your heating system and restore optimal performance.

⸻

Signs Your System Needs Power Flushing

• Radiators cold at the bottom
• Some radiators not heating properly
• Boiler making unusual noises
• Dirty or black water when bleeding radiators
• Slow heating throughout the property
• Frequent boiler breakdowns

⸻

Benefits of Power Flushing

✔ Improved heating efficiency
✔ Faster radiator heat-up time
✔ Reduced energy bills
✔ Extended boiler lifespan
✔ Removal of sludge and debris
✔ Better system circulation

A clean heating system allows your boiler and radiators to work efficiently and reliably.

⸻

Our Power Flushing Process

• Connection of professional flushing machine
• Circulation of cleaning chemicals through the system
• Individual radiator cleaning
• Sludge removal from pipework
• System neutralisation and refill
• Addition of corrosion inhibitor

Your heating system will be left clean, balanced and operating efficiently.
